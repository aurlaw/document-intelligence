RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2024,
by and between Oakview Properties LLC ("Landlord") and James T. Hartwell and
Sarah E. Hartwell ("Tenant").

PROPERTY: 742 Evergreen Terrace, Springfield, IL 62701

§1. LEASE TERM
The lease term begins on February 1, 2024 ("Start Date") and expires on
January 31, 2025 ("End Date"), unless renewed or terminated as provided herein.

§2. RENT
Tenant agrees to pay $1,850 per month ("Monthly Rent"), due on the first day of
each month ("Rent Due Date"). A late fee of $75 shall apply if rent is not received
by the 5th of the month.

§3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant has deposited $2,775 (one and one-half months' rent) as a security deposit
with Oakview Properties LLC. This deposit will be held in a separate escrow account
at First National Bank of Springfield.

§4. UTILITIES
Tenant is responsible for all utilities including electricity, gas, water, and internet
service. Landlord shall maintain the heating system and central air conditioning.

§5. MAINTENANCE
Tenant shall keep the premises clean and in good condition. Landlord shall be
responsible for structural repairs and maintenance of major appliances provided with
the unit.

§9.1 SUBLETTING
Tenant may not sublet the premises or any portion thereof without prior written consent
from Landlord. Any unauthorized subletting shall constitute grounds for termination.

§10. PETS
One domestic cat or dog under 25 lbs is permitted with a refundable pet deposit of
$500. Tenant must provide proof of current vaccinations.

§11. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 60 days written notice prior to the
End Date. Early termination by Tenant requires payment of two months' rent as a
termination fee.
